Convergence in 𝜏 with rough forcing

Last modified: 01-11-2026.

Experiments · Theory and functions

1 Problem and exact solution

Use the same tadpole and the same infinite rough forcing as in the spatial experiment. The stem has length \(1\), the loop has length \(2\), and \[ \partial_tu+\nu_\alpha L^{\alpha/2}u=g,\qquad u(0)=0, \qquad L=\kappa^2-\Delta_\Gamma,\quad \kappa=4,\quad T=1. \] The forcing is fixed across all meshes and all values of \(\alpha\): \[ g=\sum_{j=1}^{\infty}\frac1j\phi_j, \qquad \phi_j(s)= \begin{cases} 0,&s\text{ on the stem},\\ \sin(2^j\pi x),&s=x\in[0,2]\text{ on the loop}. \end{cases} \] These orthonormal eigenfunctions have eigenvalues \(\mu_j=\kappa^2+\pi^2 2^{2j}\). Thus \(g\in L_2(\Gamma)\) but \(g\notin\dot H^s(\Gamma)\) for every \(s>0\). Writing \(a_j=\nu_\alpha\mu_j^{\alpha/2}\), the exact solution is \[ u(s,t)=\sum_{j=1}^{\infty}\frac{1-e^{-a_jt}}{j\,a_j}\phi_j(s). \]

Backward Euler has first-order temporal convergence. Here we measure \[ E(\tau)=\|u-U_{h,m}^{\tau}\|_{L_2(0,T;L_2(\Gamma))} \] against the exact continuum solution, including its unresolved infinite tail. For each \(\alpha\), the mesh, rational order, and diffusion coefficient remain fixed as \(\tau\) varies. They are chosen so that spatial and rational errors remain small compared with the temporal error over the reported range. The reference slope is \(1\) for every \(\alpha\).

2 Matrix discretization and settings

To compare temporal rates on the same time grid, choose \[ \nu_\alpha=(\kappa^2+4\pi^2)^{-\alpha/2}, \qquad a_1=\nu_\alpha\mu_1^{\alpha/2}=1. \] This puts the slowest forced mode on the same time scale for every \(\alpha\). The diffusion coefficient is held fixed along each curve. This temporal experiment uses these coefficients instead of the unit diffusion used in the spatial experiment.

The solver uses consistent P1 mass and stiffness matrices, analytic sine–hat loads, and shifted sparse solves at every backward Euler step. The matrices are assembled directly on the invariant quarter-loop \([0,1/2]\), with Dirichlet endpoints. Signed reflections recover the full loop solution and the stem solution is zero. A factor of four in the matrices and integrals accounts for the full graph. This is the same FEM matrix evolution used in the spatial experiment; it uses no discrete eigenfunction recurrence.

The rational inverse uses positive BRASIL coefficients from rSPDE, including its constant term. All shifted factorizations are reused within a run. The error uses the consistent mass norm, a stable spatial projection residual, and exact time moments for the right-constant reconstruction. Modes \(j\geq J\) on a mesh \(h=2^{-J}\) have zero load but remain in the exact error through a certified infinite-tail sum. There is no reference-grid sampling or replacement of the forcing by a finite series.
